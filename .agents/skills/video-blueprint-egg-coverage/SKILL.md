# Video Blueprint: Egg Coverage Test

## Master Video Production Prompt
A vertical (9:16) macro side-by-side product comparison testing tinted sunscreens on raw brown eggs to demonstrate coverage and finish. Top-down bird's-eye camera view over a clean, matte white desk surface. Bright studio lighting illuminates two brown eggs held upright by clean, bare hands. Text overlay at the top reads "Coverage Test" with labels "Other Tinted SPF" on the left and "Tinted Dayscreen" on the right. Smooth, controlled movement as product is squeezed onto each egg and blended using black makeup cushion puffs. Pristine, minimal aesthetic, cinematic clarity, ultra-crisp detail.

## Hook & Value Proposition
- **The Hook**: Visual curiosity and unexpected surface choice (testing makeup coverage directly on textured brown eggshells).
- **The Value**: A direct, no-fluff comparison showing how the target product blurs texture, evenness, and speckles compared to a generic tinted SPF.

## Second-by-Second Scene Breakdown
- **0:00 - 0:01 | The Setup**: Hands hold two brown eggs upright over a pure white background. Top text overlays display "Coverage Test", "Other Tinted SPF", and "Tinted Dayscreen". Camera: Direct top-down (90° bird’s-eye macro view).
- **0:01 - 0:02 | Product Application**: Left hand squeezes a generic tinted SPF onto the left egg; right hand squeezes target product onto the right egg. Camera: Direct top-down, tight focus on drops landing on shells.
- **0:03 - 0:05 | The Blend Test**: Two hands simultaneously pat down product using black teardrop cushion puffs, working top to bottom. Camera: Top-down, slightly zoomed macro capturing texture absorption.
- **0:05 - 0:06 | The Reveal**: Finished eggs held up to camera. Left egg remains textured and spotty; right egg has smooth, radiant, pore-blurred finish. Text overlay changes to: "The egg has spoken 🥚".

## Lighting Configuration
- **Style**: High-key commercial lighting to eliminate harsh shadows and emphasize liquid textures and shell pores.
- **Key Light**: 200W-300W LED light positioned directly above camera setup, softened with a large (36" to 48") softbox diffusion.
- **Fill/Side Lighting**: Dual white foam boards placed on left and right sides of white table surface to wrap neutral light around eggs.
- **Color Temperature**: Daylight neutral (~5600K).

## Camera & Hardware
- **Camera**: 4K mirrorless (Sony FX3 / A7S III or Canon R5) on C-stand boom.
- **Lens**: 50mm f/2.8 Macro or 90mm f/2.8 Macro lens.
- **Settings**: f/5.6 - f/8, 1/120s @ 60fps, ISO 100 - 400.
- **Props**: Two brown eggs with natural speckles, product tube, generic squeeze-tube, two black cushion makeup puffs.
