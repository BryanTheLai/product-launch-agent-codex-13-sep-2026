# Video Blueprint: ASMR Korean Bingsu Dessert Recipe

## Master Video Production Prompt
A vertical (9:16) ASMR creative beauty recipe video mimicking a Korean red bean bingsu (shaved ice dessert) using skincare products. Set on a shimmering, translucent pink silk fabric background scattered with raw red adzuki beans. A hand scoops a purple clay mask with an ice cream scoop into a glass dessert bowl, tops it with clear gel, dispenses serum, adds ice cubes, sprinkles red beans on top, and inserts a clear spoon. Soft pastel lighting, playful pop-art sound effects, high-sensory visual aesthetics.

## Hook & Value Proposition
- **The Hook**: Playful dessert visual concept paired with text: *"let's make red bean bingsu for your pores 🍨"*.
- **The Value**: Highlights key active botanical ingredients across multiple products while demonstrating distinct textures (clay mask, gel moisturizer, fluid serum) in an engaging ASMR format.

## Second-by-Second Scene Breakdown
- **0:00 - 0:01 | The Finished Bingsu Hook**: Opening hero shot of completed "skincare bingsu" bowl with text overlay: *"let's make red bean bingsu for your pores 🍨"*. Camera: 45° high-angle close-up.
- **0:01 - 0:03 | Step 1: Base Clay Mask Scoop**: Hand holds mask jar, opens it, and uses metal ice cream scoop to place dense scoops into glass bowl. Camera: 45° high-angle macro shot tracking scoop.
- **0:04 - 0:06 | Step 2: Water Gel Topping**: Hand squeezes clear hydrating gel directly over clay scoops like dessert syrup. Camera: 45° tight macro focus on gel flow.
- **0:07 - 0:08 | Step 3: Pore Firming Serum Drizzle**: Hand squeezes dropper of serum over scoops. Camera: Extreme close-up macro on dropper tip.
- **0:09 - 0:11 | Step 4: Garnish & Final Assembly**: Clear ice cubes added, raw adzuki beans sprinkled, and clear glass spoon inserted. Camera: 45° high-angle master shot.

## Lighting Configuration
- **Style**: High-key, pastel commercial lighting creating bright reflections on glass surfaces and silk textures.
- **Key Light**: 200W daylight LED with large softbox placed at 45° top-front angle.
- **Fill Light**: White reflector opposite key light to soften shadows around glass bowl base.
- **Background Accent Light**: Low-intensity soft LED panel hitting pink silk cloth to create glistening highlights.
- **Color Temperature**: Neutral daylight (~5600K).

## Camera & Hardware
- **Camera**: Smartphone or mirrorless (Sony A7S III / FX3) on counterbalanced boom arm.
- **Lens**: 50mm f/2.8 Macro or 90mm Macro Lens.
- **Settings**: 60 fps, 1/120s, f/4 - f/5.6, ISO 100 - 300.
- **Props**: Glass footed dessert bowl, mechanical metal ice cream scoop, clear glass spoon, raw red adzuki beans, acrylic clear ice cubes, shimmering pink silk backdrop.
