# Video Blueprint: Underwater Micro-Bubble Hydration

## Master Video Production Prompt
A vertical (9:16) commercial product shot featuring sunscreen/moisturizer tube surrounded by an underwater cascade of fine, rising air bubbles. Soft blue-tinted aquatic lighting highlights moisture droplets clinging to the matte white tube and bold typography. Fluid vertical camera pans and dynamic cuts shift between close-up details of the packaging, flowing water streams, and rising bubbles. Refreshing, ultra-clean aesthetic with hyper-detailed fluid dynamics and high-speed clarity.

## Hook & Value Proposition
- **The Hook**: High-speed underwater visual with dense, rising micro-bubbles and clear flowing water to immediately visually signal ultra-lightweight, splash-like hydration.
- **The Value**: Highlights the refreshing, weightless, and hydrating nature of the formula compared to traditional heavy products.

## Second-by-Second Scene Breakdown
- **0:00 - 0:01 | Micro-Bubble Fluid Hook**: Camera pans vertically over the inverted sunscreen tube engulfed in a dense bed of rising micro-bubbles. Camera: Close-up macro (45° diagonal sweep), low depth of field.
- **0:01 - 0:03 | Product Reveal & Water Droplets**: Fast cut to clean, centered shot of upright product tube as water trickles down its sides and droplets form on the surface. Camera: Straight-on eye-level macro shot centered on product branding.
- **0:03 - 0:04 | Bubble Cascade Transition**: High-speed transition through dense aquatic bubbles rushing upward, clearing screen. Camera: Extreme macro tracking shot following bubble stream.
- **0:04 - 0:06 | Dynamic Water Flow**: Fast cuts alternating between diagonal close-ups and full product shots while stream of water rushes over package surface. Camera: Alternate between 45° low-angle macro and direct front-facing vertical framing.
- **0:06 - 0:07 | Final Product Hero Shot**: Clean front view of tube locked in place as water streams continuously past it in slow motion. Camera: Locked central eye-level portrait shot.

## Lighting Configuration
- **Style**: Aquatic high-key backlight combined with crisp directional key lighting to make water droplets and air bubbles glisten.
- **Key Light**: 300W daylight LED with parabolic softbox placed at 45° top-front angle.
- **Backlight / Underwater Accent**: High-intensity LED panel positioned behind transparent acrylic water tank, gelled with cool daylight white (~6000K).
- **Fill Light**: White reflector board positioned below camera to soften deep shadows on bottom text.

## Camera & Hardware
- **Camera**: High-speed cinema camera (Sony FX6, RED Komodo, or Sony A7S III) capable of 4K at 120fps.
- **Lens**: 90mm or 100mm f/2.8 Macro Lens.
- **Rigging**: Vertical C-stand boom or motorized slider.
- **Settings**: 120 fps, 1/250s - 1/500s, f/4 - f/5.6, ISO 200 - 400.
- **Props**: Custom acrylic clear water tank, submersible air bubbler diffuser bar, hydrophobic coating spray.
