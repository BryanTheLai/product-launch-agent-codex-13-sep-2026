# Video Blueprint: Problem-to-Solution Invisible Skin Swatch

## Master Video Production Prompt
A vertical (9:16) direct-response sunscreen comparison and demo video set in a bright indoor room with natural window lighting. Begins with a three-photo collage of common sunscreen issues (clogged pores, white cast, greasy shine) before transitioning to hand-held product reveals and close-up skin application swatches. Direct, clean, lifestyle beauty aesthetic focusing on lightweight texture, fast absorption, and invisible finishing.

## Hook & Value Proposition
- **The Hook**: Problem-solution collage addressing common consumer pain points with text: *"If you hate SPF because it looks like this..."* followed immediately by a solution reveal: *"try this instead ✨"*.
- **The Value**: Reassures users who struggle with heavy, greasy, or pore-clogging formulas by demonstrating a zero-weight, fast-absorbing formula that leaves a natural, invisible finish.

## Second-by-Second Scene Breakdown
- **0:00 - 0:01 | Problem Hook Collage**: Static frame showing three close-up photos of common skin problems (clogged pores, white cast, greasy sheen). Overlay: *"If you hate SPF because it looks like this..."*. Camera: Graphic overlay on ambient indoor background.
- **0:01 - 0:02 | The Solution Reveal**: Hand holds up product container into frame. Overlay: *"try this instead ✨"*. Camera: Eye-level handheld medium shot.
- **0:02 - 0:03 | Texture Dispense (Palm)**: Product dispenses smoothly into open palm, showing creamy, light texture. Overlay: *"✅ moisturizer with spf"*. Camera: High-angle macro shot focused on palm.
- **0:03 - 0:04 | Texture Dispense (Back of Hand)**: Product dispenses across back of hand. Overlay: *"✅ zero-weight hydration"*. Camera: 45° macro close-up.
- **0:04 - 0:05 | Application & Blending**: Fingers rub lotion into skin; instantly turns translucent without white cast. Overlay: *"✅ feels like wearing nothing"*. Camera: 45° macro close-up tracking motion.
- **0:05 - 0:06 | Invisible Finish Reveal**: Hand turns under natural window light, showing soft, non-greasy, natural skin finish. Overlay: *"✅ barely-there finish"*. Camera: Locked macro shot.
- **0:06 - 0:07 | Final Product & Skin Framing**: Hand holds container alongside blended hand. Overlay: *"skip everything you don't love -> keep what matters ✨"*. Camera: Medium macro shot.

## Lighting Configuration
- **Style**: Soft, natural indoor daylight (window key light) giving a realistic, lifestyle feel.
- **Key Light**: Large natural side-window light coming from left/top-left (~5500K daylight).
- **Fill Light**: Ambient room reflection / white wall on right to keep shadows soft.

## Camera & Hardware
- **Camera**: Handheld smartphone or mirrorless (4K 60fps).
- **Lens**: 35mm to 50mm Prime lens.
- **Settings**: 60 fps, 1/120s, f/2.8 - f/4, ISO 100 - 400.
- **Props**: Product container, clean hands, indoor room plant/lamp background.
