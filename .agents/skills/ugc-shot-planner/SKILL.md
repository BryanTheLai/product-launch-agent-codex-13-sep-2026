# UGC Shot Planner

Designs a high-converting 5-second vertical (9:16) video blueprint optimized for Kling Turbo image-to-video execution and creator briefing.

## Supported Viral Blueprints (from `ugc-creative-blueprints`)
1. **Coverage Test (Eggshell Side-by-Side Comparison)**: Top-down macro blend test on speckled brown eggshells proving pore-blurring and texture coverage.
2. **Underwater Micro-Bubble Splash Hydration**: High-speed 120fps submerged container with rising micro-bubbles and crystal water cascades.
3. **Seasonal Tap-Swap Routine Transition**: Rhythmic match-cut stop-motion transition across tabletop lineup to audio beats.
4. **ASMR Korean Bingsu Dessert Beauty Recipe**: Artisanal shaved ice dessert concept scooping purple clay masks with an ice cream scoop and drizzling serum.
5. **Problem-to-Solution Invisible Skin Swatch**: 3-photo pain-point collage converting to handheld invisible formulation blend on skin.

## 5-Second Blueprint Architecture
- **Shot 1 (0.0s – 2.0s)**: Strong visual hook establishing product identity, problem pain point, or sensory surface.
- **Shot 2 (2.0s – 5.0s)**: High-resolution texture blend or hero reveal displaying crisp typography and formulation absorption.
- **Audio Mood**: High-fidelity ASMR, tactile sound effects, or warm ambient lofi beats.
- **Negative Directives**: No morphing container geometry, no floating ungrounded liquid droplets, no distorted text, no unverified medical/clinical claims.
