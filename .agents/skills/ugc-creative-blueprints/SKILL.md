# UGC Creative Production Blueprints

A library of 5 reverse-engineered, high-converting video blueprints tailored for cosmetic, skincare, and beauty DTC products. Each blueprint provides exact hook mechanics, second-by-second shot choreography, lighting specifications, camera settings, and Kling Turbo video prompts.

---

## 1. Blueprint 1: Coverage Test (Eggshell Side-by-Side Comparison)
- **Format**: `comparison_macro`
- **Ideal For**: Tinted sunscreens, tone-up creams, skin tints, BB/CC creams, mineral coverage.
- **The Hook**: Testing makeup coverage directly on textured, speckled brown eggshells.
- **Hook Copy**: *"Coverage Test: The egg has spoken 🥚"*
- **Value Proposition**: Direct, zero-fluff comparison demonstrating how the target formula blurs texture, pores, and hyperpigmentation speckles compared to a heavy, chalky generic SPF.
- **Shot Breakdown**:
  - `0:00 - 0:01` **The Setup**: Top-down 90° bird's-eye macro view. Hands hold two speckled brown eggs upright over pure matte white surface. Overlays: "Coverage Test", "Other Tinted SPF", "Target Product".
  - `0:01 - 0:02` **Product Application**: Synchronized squeeze of generic lotion on left egg and target formula on right egg.
  - `0:03 - 0:05` **The Blend Test**: Two black teardrop cushion puffs simultaneously pat down formulas from top to bottom. Left egg remains spotty; right egg blurs seamlessly.
  - `0:05 - 0:06` **The Reveal**: Finished eggs held up to camera. Overlay: *"The egg has spoken 🥚"*.
- **Lighting**: 5600K high-key commercial lighting, 200W-300W overhead LED with 36"-48" softbox diffusion, dual white foam bounce boards.
- **Camera Rig**: 4K camera on C-stand overhead boom, 50mm or 90mm f/2.8 macro lens, f/5.6-f/8, 1/120s @ 60fps, ISO 100-400.
- **Props**: Two speckled brown eggs, black teardrop makeup cushion puffs, unbranded beige tube, target product tube.

## 2. Blueprint 2: Underwater Micro-Bubble Splash Hydration
- **Format**: `fluid_dynamics`
- **Ideal For**: Gel moisturizers, watery sunscreens, hydrating essences, hyaluronic toners, cooling serums.
- **The Hook**: High-speed underwater visual with dense, rising micro-bubbles and crystal-clear flowing water streams.
- **Hook Copy**: *"Weightless splash hydration 🌊"*
- **Value Proposition**: Visualizes weightless, non-sticky, splash-like hydration that breaks into water upon skin contact.
- **Shot Breakdown**:
  - `0:00 - 0:01` **Micro-Bubble Fluid Hook**: 45° diagonal sweep macro. Inverted product tube engulfed in a dense bed of rising micro-bubbles in clear water.
  - `0:01 - 0:03` **Product Reveal & Droplets**: Eye-level straight-on macro of upright tube with beading water droplets running down sides.
  - `0:03 - 0:04` **Bubble Cascade Transition**: High-speed tracking shot through dense bubble rush.
  - `0:04 - 0:06` **Dynamic Water Flow**: Alternating diagonal cuts as water streams continuously past packaging.
  - `0:06 - 0:07` **Final Hero Shot**: Locked central portrait with glistening specular droplet highlights.
- **Lighting**: Aquatic high-key backlight, 300W daylight LED with parabolic softbox (45° top-front), rear LED panel behind acrylic water tank (~6000K), bottom white bounce.
- **Camera Rig**: High-speed cinema camera (Sony FX6 / Komodo / A7S III) @ 120fps, 90mm/100mm f/2.8 macro, 1/250s-1/500s, f/4-f/5.6.
- **Props**: Custom clear acrylic water tank, submersible diffuser bubbler bar, hydrophobic coating spray on bottle.

## 3. Blueprint 3: Seasonal Tap-Swap Routine Transition
- **Format**: `rhythmic_transition`
- **Ideal For**: Seasonal routine resets, multi-SKU product lines, morning/evening rituals, winter skin transitions.
- **The Hook**: Relatable seasonal meme paired with satisfying match-cut snap-swapping to a musical beat.
- **Hook Copy**: *"me on September 1st 🍂🤎"*
- **Value Proposition**: Signals seasonal transition from lightweight summer hydration to rich barrier nourishment in an engaging format.
- **Shot Breakdown**:
  - `0:00 - 0:01` **Summer Lineup Setup**: 45° stationary wide shot showing 5-6 products on wooden tabletop. Hand enters from left and taps first product on beat.
  - `0:01 - 0:02` **First Snap Swap**: Hand taps 3rd product; instantly match-cuts to deep amber essence bottle on beat.
  - `0:02 - 0:03` **Second Snap Swap**: Hand taps 4th product; snaps into golden nourishing facial oil.
  - `0:03 - 0:04` **Final Swap**: Hand taps 5th product; snaps into heavy white cream jar.
  - `0:04 - 0:05` **Routine Reveal**: Hand pulls back; fall routine neatly arranged in warm afternoon light.
- **Lighting**: Warm 5000K-5500K golden hour window daylight at 45°, sheer curtain diffuser, white bounce board.
- **Camera Rig**: 4K mirrorless on rigid tripod with AE/AF lock enabled, 35mm-50mm prime, f/4, 1/120s @ 60fps.
- **Props**: Light oak wood table, neutral beige wall, ascending skincare bottles, dried botanical accent.

## 4. Blueprint 4: ASMR Korean Bingsu Dessert Beauty Recipe
- **Format**: `sensory_asmr_recipe`
- **Ideal For**: Clay masks, wash-off masks, whipped moisturizers, pore cleansers, botanical active ingredients (red bean, rice, mugwort, green tea).
- **The Hook**: Skincare presented as an artisanal Korean shaved ice dessert (Bingsu) with sensory scooping and drizzling.
- **Hook Copy**: *"let's make bingsu for your pores 🍨"*
- **Value Proposition**: Demonstrates complex, satisfying formulation textures (dense clay, translucent gel, fluid serum) in a hypnotic ASMR format.
- **Shot Breakdown**:
  - `0:00 - 0:01` **Finished Dessert Hook**: 45° high-angle hero shot of completed skincare bingsu bowl with clear spoon.
  - `0:01 - 0:03` **Clay Mask Scoop**: Metal ice cream scoop takes dense, velvety purple clay mask scoops and places into footed glass dessert bowl.
  - `0:04 - 0:06` **Water Gel Topping**: Squeezes clear hydrating gel over scoops like dessert syrup.
  - `0:07 - 0:08` **Serum Drizzle**: Glass dropper dispenses fluid serum over the top.
  - `0:09 - 0:11` **Garnish & Assembly**: Acrylic ice cubes placed, raw adzuki beans sprinkled, crystal spoon inserted.
- **Lighting**: High-key pastel commercial lighting (~5600K), 200W softbox key light, soft pink silk background illumination.
- **Camera Rig**: Mirrorless on counterbalanced boom arm, 50mm/90mm f/2.8 macro, f/4-f/5.6, 1/120s @ 60fps.
- **Props**: Footed glass dessert bowl, mechanical metal ice cream scoop, glass spoon, raw adzuki beans, acrylic ice cubes, shimmering pink silk.

## 5. Blueprint 5: Problem-to-Solution Invisible Skin Swatch
- **Format**: `problem_solution_swatch`
- **Ideal For**: Daily SPF, lightweight lotions, barrier serums, invisible finishes, non-comedogenic formulas.
- **The Hook**: Direct-response 3-photo collage of consumer sunscreen frustrations (white cast, clogged pores, greasy shine) transitioning to immediate solution reveal.
- **Hook Copy**: *"If you hate SPF because it looks like this... try this instead ✨"*
- **Value Proposition**: Directly addresses negative sunscreen experiences by proving zero white cast, fast absorption, and a natural skin finish.
- **Shot Breakdown**:
  - `0:00 - 0:01` **Problem Hook Collage**: Static 3-photo collage highlighting white cast and greasy skin.
  - `0:01 - 0:02` **Solution Reveal**: Handheld presentation of target tube into frame. Overlay: *"try this instead ✨"*.
  - `0:02 - 0:03` **Texture Dispense**: Smooth cream ribbon onto open palm and back of hand.
  - `0:04 - 0:05` **Application & Blending**: Fingers massage formula; instantly melts into translucent veil with zero white cast.
  - `0:05 - 0:06` **Invisible Finish Reveal**: Hand tilts under natural light showing breathable, natural skin texture. Overlay: *"barely-there finish ✨"*.
  - `0:06 - 0:07` **Final Framing**: Hand holds tube alongside blended skin. Overlay: *"skip what you don't love, keep what matters"*.
- **Lighting**: Soft natural indoor daylight (~5500K) from large side window with ambient wall fill.
- **Camera Rig**: Handheld 4K 60fps smartphone or mirrorless, 35mm-50mm prime, f/2.8-f/4, 1/120s.
- **Props**: Target product container, clean hands, natural indoor background plant/lamp.
